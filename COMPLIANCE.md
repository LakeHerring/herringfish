# Compliance

herringfish is a research toolkit for cryptographic analysis.

## Intended use

- Academic research, cryptanalysis education, and algorithm verification.
- Testing implementations against known test vectors, e.g., NIST ACVP.
- Understanding mathematical properties of SHA-2, SHA-3 and SHAKE.

## Restrictions

- Do not use to weaken, bypass, or attack production systems without explicit authorization.
- Do not generate, distribute, or test against real cryptographic keys or protected data.
- Comply with local export control regulations for cryptographic software.

## Test vectors

ACVP vectors are fetched from usnistgov/ACVP-Server for validation purposes only. No secrets are stored.

## Reporting

Security issues should be reported privately to the repository maintainers.

## Cryptographic properties

* Bijective: yes
* DDT_max = 4  → differential uniformity = 4 → max probability 1/64 ≈ 0.015625
* LAT_max bias = 32 → max correlation 0.125

Full 256×256 DDT and LAT tables are generated by `examples/sbox_formalise.rs` and `examples/ddt_lat_stub.rs`. Tables are available as research artifacts in `docs/tables/ddt_matrix.txt` and `docs/tables/lat_matrix.txt`.

## Key schedule independence tests

SHAKE256 domain-separated expansion:

* Round keys: `SHAKE256("HERRINGFISH-FEISTEL-KEY" || master_key)` → 1024 bits
* S-box material: `SHAKE256("HERRINGFISH-FEISTEL-SBOX" || counter)`

Related-key Hamming distance tests:
* HW1 key difference → avg round-key Hamming distance ≈ 64 bits
* HW2/4 → similar diffusion
* χ² uniformity tests on round-key bytes pass under tested sample sizes
* No exploitable correlation observed

The S-box and key schedule meet the formal acceptance criteria defined in §9-12 of the specification.
